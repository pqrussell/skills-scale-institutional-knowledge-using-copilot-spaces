# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This folder is the central reference point for how OctoAcme plans, delivers, releases, and improves cross-functional work. The process is customer-first, emphasizes iterative delivery in small, testable increments, assigns clear ownership, uses data to guide decisions, and promotes psychological safety so teams can raise risks early and learn continuously.

At a high level, OctoAcme projects move through five connected phases: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective improvement. Teams validate the problem and success metrics up front, break work into a prioritized backlog, manage delivery through a visible project board, release through a standard checklist, and capture follow-up actions after each sprint, milestone, or incident.

## Process overview

- **Overview & Principles**: Keep customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety at the center of project work.
- **Execution & Quality Workflows**: Track work on a project board with Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done columns; prefer small PRs; hold regular standups and delivery syncs; and build quality into each layer with unit tests, integration tests, smoke tests, security scanning, and manual QA.
- **Risk Management & Communication**: Maintain a structured risk register, use a three-level escalation path for blockers, and send weekly status updates with a consistent template.
- **Release & Continuous Improvement**: Use standard patch, minor, and major release types; complete pre-release checks; and run retrospectives that produce a short list of prioritized improvements.

## Project lifecycle

1. **Initiation**: Validate the problem, align stakeholders, and define success criteria.
2. **Planning**: Break work into shippable increments, identify dependencies, and agree on milestones.
3. **Execution & Tracking**: Deliver through regular standups, reviews, QA, and progress tracking.
4. **Release & Deployment**: Verify readiness, deploy safely, and communicate the release.
5. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**: Capture learnings, assign actions, and improve the next cycle.

## Core roles quick reference

- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery, timelines, risks, dependencies, and stakeholder communication.
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes the roadmap and backlog, and measures success.
- **Developers**: Design, implement, test, review, and deliver reliable software increments.
- **QA/Testing**: Validates acceptance criteria, test coverage, and release readiness.
- **Stakeholders / Sponsors**: Provide business context, approvals, and escalation support when needed.

- **If you are new to OctoAcme processes**: Read the overview first, then the roles guide, then the lifecycle document for your current phase.
