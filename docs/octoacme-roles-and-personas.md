# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads ensure product quality by defining testing strategies, validating acceptance criteria, and coordinating quality assurance activities across features and releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test approach for features, milestones, and releases
- Partner with developers and Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Coordinate manual QA, automated coverage, and release readiness checks
- Identify quality risks, defects, and regression trends early
- Report test status and quality signals to Project Managers and stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure delivered work meets quality expectations before release
- Reduce escaped defects and release risk
- Build confidence in releases through repeatable test coverage and clear sign-off

### Typical Communication
- Sprint / iteration planning and backlog refinement with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Test plans, bug triage, and QA status updates with developers
- Release readiness reviews and acceptance sign-off for stakeholders

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects provide architectural guidance, mentor developers, and ensure implementation decisions support OctoAcme's long-term system design, maintainability, and scalability goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approaches, architecture boundaries, and integration patterns for major work
- Review designs and implementation plans with developers before and during delivery
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and trade-offs for Project Managers and Product Managers
- Guide engineering standards, observability, and maintainability practices
- Mentor developers and support technical decision-making across the team

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are scalable, reliable, and maintainable
- Reduce technical debt and avoid short-term decisions that create long-term risk
- Strengthen team capability through mentoring and clear technical direction

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, architecture notes, and implementation walkthroughs with developers
- Trade-off discussions and roadmap impact reviews with Product Managers
- Risk, dependency, and sequencing conversations with Project Managers

---

## Security Champions

### Role Summary
Security Champions embed security practices into day-to-day delivery by identifying risks early, promoting secure implementation patterns, and ensuring compliance expectations are addressed throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review proposed work for security risks, sensitive data handling, and compliance needs
- Lead or facilitate threat assessments for higher-risk changes
- Define security acceptance criteria with Product Managers and developers
- Coordinate security scanning, remediation follow-up, and incident communication paths
- Promote secure coding, dependency hygiene, and least-privilege practices across the team

### Goals
- Prevent avoidable security issues from reaching production
- Ensure security requirements are included in planning, testing, and release decisions
- Build a team culture where security is part of normal delivery work

### Typical Communication
- Planning and design checkpoints with Technical Leads / Architects and developers
- Security review findings and remediation tracking with Project Managers
- Incident escalation and compliance updates with stakeholders and security partners

---

## Project Sponsors / Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors / Stakeholders represent business priorities, provide funding or organizational support, and make strategic decisions that keep delivery aligned with expected outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define business context, desired outcomes, and success measures with Product Managers
- Approve major scope, timeline, budget, and priority trade-offs
- Remove organizational blockers and help secure resources when escalation is needed
- Review milestones, risks, and release readiness with Project Managers
- Provide approvals, feedback, and direction at key decision points

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers meaningful business value
- Keep teams aligned on priorities, constraints, and expected outcomes
- Reduce business risk by making timely decisions and supporting escalations

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff meetings, milestone reviews, and regular stakeholder updates
- Decision logs, status reports, and escalation checkpoints with Project Managers
- Outcome reviews, demos, and priority discussions with Product Managers

---

## Scrum Masters / Iteration Leads

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Iteration Leads facilitate the team's delivery rhythm, help remove impediments, and ensure planning, execution, and retrospective ceremonies support continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint / iteration planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team maintain a healthy backlog flow and clear work-in-progress priorities
- Surface blockers, dependencies, and process issues to Project Managers and Technical Leads / Architects
- Coach the team on agreed ways of working, estimation, and continuous improvement
- Support coordination between developers, Product Managers, and QA / Testing Leads during delivery

### Goals
- Maintain a predictable and sustainable delivery cadence
- Reduce friction in team processes and speed up blocker resolution
- Help the team improve collaboration, focus, and follow-through each iteration

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups, sprint / iteration planning, demos, and retrospectives
- Board hygiene, blocker follow-up, and dependency coordination with Project Managers
- Working agreements, team health discussions, and process coaching with the delivery team
